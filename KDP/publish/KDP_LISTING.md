## Plain-text description (fallback if not using description.html)

Building Vision AI is a practitioner's guide to computer vision in 39 chapters
across four parts: image processing, classical computer vision, deep learning for
vision, and generative vision models. Every core idea is built from scratch and
then shown with the modern library that makes it a few lines of code. The Second
Edition adds full graduate-level derivations across the generative half: the
variational lower bound and its rate-distortion view, energy-based models and
score matching, the diffusion SDEs and probability-flow ODE, classifier-free
guidance, flow matching and consistency models, latent-dynamics and generative
world models, and joint-embedding predictive architectures, with a 13-week
graduate course map. With 244 illustrations, runnable code, hands-on labs, and
exercises, it takes you from your first NumPy pixel manipulation to fine-tuning
diffusion models and building world models, told as one connected story.
