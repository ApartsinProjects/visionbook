Build computer vision from first principles to generative models.

Building Vision AI is a practitioner's guide that takes you from your first NumPy pixel manipulation to fine-tuning diffusion models, told as one connected story across four parts and 39 chapters.

Every core idea is built from scratch, then shown with the modern library that turns it into a few lines of code, with 244 illustrations, inline diagrams, runnable code, hands-on labs, and exercises throughout.

What you'll learn:

  • Image processing — spatial filtering, the frequency domain, geometric warping, and morphology.
  • Classical computer vision — features, matching, camera models, and multi-view geometry.
  • Deep learning for vision — convolutional networks, vision transformers, object detection, and segmentation.
  • Self-supervised foundation models — CLIP, DINO, and MAE.
  • 3D and scenes — monocular depth, NeRF, and 3D Gaussian splatting.
  • Generative vision — variational autoencoders, GANs, diffusion and text-to-image systems, controllable generation and editing, and video and world models.
  • Doing it responsibly — honest evaluation, synthetic data, and governance.

Who it's for: engineers, researchers, and students who want to understand modern vision systems, not just call an API.
